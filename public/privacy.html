Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Information We Collect

Content Matrix collects only the information necessary to provide its services, including TikTok account credentials (via OAuth) and video metadata.

2. TikTok Data

When you connect your TikTok account, we store an access token to upload videos on your behalf. We do not store your TikTok password. We access only the scopes you explicitly grant: video.publish, video.upload, and user.info.basic.

3. Data Storage

Video metadata and job status are stored locally in a database on your own server. We do not transmit your data to third parties except TikTok (for publishing) and YouTube (for publishing).

4. Data Deletion

You can delete your data at any time by removing jobs from the dashboard. You can revoke TikTok API access via your TikTok account settings under "Manage app permissions."

5. Contact

For privacy questions, visit: content-matrix-sigma.vercel.app
